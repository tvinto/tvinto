# The Tvinto name and logo

The code is free to use under its licences. The name **Tvinto** and its logo
(the "i" with a play arrow as its dot, in `brand/`) are not covered by those
licences. They show which copy is the official one, so people know what
they're installing.

## You can

- Build, use and change Tvinto for yourself, and run your own sync service,
  keeping the name and logo as they are.
- Say your project is "based on Tvinto", "a fork of Tvinto" or "works with
  Tvinto".
- Use the name and logo to write about the project, link to it or review it.

## Please ask first before you

- Publish a changed copy under the name Tvinto, or with its logo or an icon
  that looks like it. That includes app stores, downloads and hosted
  services. Give your copy its own name and icon.
- Present something as the official Tvinto, or as approved by the project,
  when it isn't.
- Use the name or logo for anything that sells or bundles channels, playlists
  or subscriptions. Tvinto is only a player.

If you're not sure, open an issue and ask.
